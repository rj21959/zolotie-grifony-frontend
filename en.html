Golden Griffin

Volunteer Development Center

English name: Golden Griffin (also known as Golden Griffon)

Russian name: Золотые Грифоны (Zolotie Grifony)

Location: Novy Urengoy, Yamalo-Nenets Autonomous Okrug, Russia

Founded: 2019

Type: Non-profit organization (ANO)

About Us

Golden Griffin is a regional volunteer center for school and family volunteering, based in Novy Urengoy, Russia. We unite people who want to change the world for the better through volunteer work and social activity.

Our Directions

  • Ecological volunteering
  • Social assistance
  • Event organization
  • Animal protection
  • Sports and health
  • Patriotic education
